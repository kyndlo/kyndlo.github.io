Website analytics

Updated October 8, 2026. This notice describes optional analytics across the FiveOrbit Studio website, app pages and download pages, not data collected inside our apps.

If you choose Allow analytics, Google Analytics measures page visits, referral sources, advertising campaign labels app exploration, contact-button clicks and store-button clicks or automatic store redirects. App and store labels identify which product was selected. Google receives technical information including your IP address and browser/device information when your browser connects to its service. Analytics may store cookies. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in this website integration.

We only load Google Analytics after you allow it. You can change your choice using Analytics choices on any website or download page. Declining does not affect downloads. Changing your choice stops future analytics collection; it does not remove previously collected data or existing browser cookies.

We save your analytics preference locally in your browser. Campaign labels from marketing links are retained for the browser-tab session and included in links to the app stores so store acquisition reports can identify the campaign. These labels should contain campaign names, never personal information.

Store-button clicks are not proof of an installation. Apple and Google independently operate their stores and provide aggregate acquisition reports.
