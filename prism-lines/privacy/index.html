Prism Lines privacy policy

Updated November 12, 2026

Prism Lines is published by Kyndlo LLC. This policy covers the iOS and native Android apps, including the Meta campaign-measurement and FiveOrbit gameplay-analytics integrations. You can play without creating a Kyndlo account.

Game data on your device

Progress, settings, earned rewards, saved powers and purchase entitlement records are stored on your device. Local purchase receipts prevent duplicate delivery. Kyndlo does not operate a server that receives your board contents or saved games. Device backups and optional cosmetic sync may be processed by Apple or Google under your account settings.

If you enable daily reminders, the app asks for notification permission and schedules reminders locally. Reminder preferences are not sent to a Kyndlo server.

Game Center, Google Play Games and purchases

Optional Apple Game Center and Google Play Games features use those platform services for sign-in, achievements, leaderboards and supported cosmetic preferences. Their processing is governed by Apple and Google and your account settings. We do not send Game Center or Play Games player identifiers to Meta.

Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing process purchases. The app verifies purchase records to provide or restore entitlements and deliver consumable packs once. Kyndlo does not receive payment-card or banking information. Consumable saved-power inventory may be device-local and is separate from restorable ad-free entitlements.

Advertising and optional video rewards

Eligible free players may see a small banner in the reserved bottom area during gameplay. Banners are removed or paused while menus, celebrations or reward videos are presented. Free players may also choose a rewarded video for an in-game benefit, such as a recovery, bonus fragments that advance saved-power rewards, or a Fire head start. You may decline and continue by replaying or starting another level. A verified ad-free entitlement disables both banner and rewarded advertisements.

The current native integration uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Google AdMob bidding, plus LevelPlay Ad Quality. InMobi Choice gathers applicable privacy choices before ad requests. Ads require resolved privacy choices and verified purchase ownership. Earlier installed versions may use Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform directly.

Advertising and consent partners may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device/app/network information, consent preferences and ad interactions for delivery, fraud prevention, measurement and applicable personalization. Precise location, contacts, camera and microphone access are not requested for advertising.

Meta campaign measurement

The Meta (Facebook) Core SDK measures which ads bring people to Prism Lines and which campaigns lead to engaged play, purchases and advertising revenue. It does not serve the game's ads. Kyndlo can use campaign reports to evaluate and improve advertising.

When the app's privacy gate permits collection, Meta may receive device and advertising identifiers, IP address, device/app information, installs and app activations, tutorial completions, completed levels, element unlocks, purchase events, and ad impression events with estimated revenue, ad network, format and placement. Revenue is reported from the ad impression, even if a video is not completed; missing or invalid revenue is not reported as earnings.

The integration does not supply names, email addresses, phone numbers, Game Center or Play Games player identifiers, or board contents to Meta. Automatic advanced matching and automatic additional metadata collection are disabled. App links opened from Meta ads may be handled by the SDK for attribution.

Meta Core initializes at app launch with automatic event logging and advertising-ID collection disabled. The app then applies applicable CMP choices, including saved choices on returning visits. In GDPR regions, device-access and advertising-measurement purpose consent are required, and an explicit decline for the Meta measurement vendor blocks app-initiated events. On iOS, Meta's SDK reads the actual App Tracking Transparency status and cannot obtain IDFA when tracking is not authorized. Declining ATT does not by itself disable all limited app-event measurement supported by the SDK. A supported US sale/sharing opt-out or Global Privacy Control is passed to Meta as Limited Data Use; an unresolved US choice also uses that restricted processing. Unknown applicability or declined or withdrawn required purpose choices block new app-initiated Meta events. Editing privacy choices pauses automatic event collection while the form is visible. Previously received partner data remains subject to the partner's retention and deletion controls.

FiveOrbit gameplay analytics

When applicable choices permit it, the app sends gameplay events to Kyndlo's FiveOrbit EventHub at dailyspirit.azurewebsites.net. We use these reports to understand activity and return visits, improve level difficulty and element reachability, and check purchases and advertising reliability and reward usefulness.

The client creates a random install identifier and a random session identifier. These are not derived from IDFA, Android advertising ID, device hardware IDs or account identifiers, and are not sent to Meta or joined with Meta advertising identifiers. Deleting app data creates a new install identifier.

Events include the first consented app open and return milestones; mode, level and material; tutorial completions, level starts, completions and failures, board clears, active play seconds and action counts; reward offers, starts, displayed videos, earned rewards, closes and failures; checkout starts and verified new purchase counts. Active play excludes menus, ads and background time and stops after 60 seconds without a move. No board contents, player-entered text, names, email addresses or payment-card details are included.

Events wait in a bounded local queue while offline: at most 500 events, kept for no more than seven days. Batches use encrypted HTTPS. Required consent withdrawal stops new collection, cancels outstanding requests where possible and removes unsent events. The event store does not record IP addresses or user agents from these gameplay requests; network infrastructure still processes connection information to deliver them.

EventHub currently retains raw gameplay events for 400 days and then removes them on its cleanup schedule. Dashboard figures describe participating installations and sessions, not every store download. We do not sell these records or share the EventHub install identifier with advertising networks.

GDPR collection requires separate FiveOrbit gameplay-analytics vendor consent, device-access purpose consent and content-measurement purpose consent. The iOS integration also honors a declined App Tracking Transparency choice by keeping this client off. A supported US sale/sharing opt-out also keeps this client off. Meta campaign measurement and FiveOrbit gameplay analytics have separate consent gates.

Additional events describe level attempts, element introductions and voice-line identifiers, actual material use, ad opportunities and saved reward grants, fragment and Rotate progress, gifts, badges, product views and experiment exposure. Random opportunity and ad-attempt identifiers connect stages of a reward flow. Reports distinguish test and production builds, ad format and estimated revenue precision. Where a verified store transaction provides an amount and currency, those values may accompany the purchase event; catalog prices are not treated as verified proceeds. Bounded, stable local experiment assignments help compare presentation and banner availability without changing gameplay rules.

A minimal first-seen and last-seen installation cohort record is kept separately from raw events so routine event cleanup does not make an active returning installation appear new. Cohort records expire after the configured retention period of inactivity (currently 400 days) and are removed by applicable installation or game-data deletion requests. These records contain no advertising or account identifier.

Your privacy choices

Use Privacy Options in the app's Settings or Shop to review or change available InMobi choices, including separate Meta campaign-measurement and FiveOrbit gameplay-analytics vendors where GDPR applies. On iOS, you can also change tracking permission in system Settings. Android advertising-ID controls are available in system Settings. Declining measurement does not prevent playing, earning ordinary rewards or purchasing.

Ad-free ownership disables banners and rewarded videos; it does not replace separate measurement choices. Optional FiveOrbit website analytics has its own notice and consent controls.

Children and family controls

Prism Lines does not request names, email addresses, precise location or other contact details from children for gameplay. Advertising and measurement remain subject to the privacy gates above. Parents and guardians can manage app access, tracking and purchases through Apple and Android family controls.

Retention, access and deletion

Local game data remains until removed through available app controls, deletion of the app, or backups you control. Deleting the app stops that installation from sending events, but does not automatically delete previously received EventHub or partner records. EventHub's raw-event retention is described above. Apple, Google, Meta and the advertising/consent providers set their own retention periods and provide account, privacy and deletion controls.

For access, correction or deletion questions about Prism Lines, contact carlos@kyndlo.com. Please identify the app and platform. EventHub records are associated with random install identifiers rather than names or accounts, so we may need that app-generated identifier to locate them. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or identifiers by public issue. Meta account activity and advertising choices can also be managed through Meta's own privacy tools.

Policy changes

We update this notice and applicable App Store privacy and Google Play Data safety disclosures when the app's data practices change. Updated integrations are described here before their release.
