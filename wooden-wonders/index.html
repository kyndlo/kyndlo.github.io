The feeling

Slow down. Find the shape.

Wooden Wonders turns a familiar puzzle into a tactile little world. Move pieces around a table, try a different material, and let the final sculpture settle wherever you built it.

Designed for landscape play on iPhone and iPad, with 26 paintings, sculpted silhouettes, magnetic joins, and a generous table made for exploring.
