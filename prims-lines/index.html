The loop

Place a piece. Clear a line. Keep going.

Drag a piece onto the board — or tap it, then tap a cell. Complete horizontal and vertical lines to clear them and reach each level goal.

The piece library grows with you, adding new shapes without overwhelming the opening moves. Progress, score, combo, and the active board are saved as you play.
